# Embodied AI, Robotics & Autonomous Driving — Oct 2025 to Oct 2026

*Depth survey for the comprehensive AI/ML review. Scope: vision-language-action (VLA) models, robot manipulation & locomotion, humanoids, navigation, sim-to-real, world models for control, RL for robots, autonomous driving (end-to-end and VLM-based), and embodied benchmarks. Weighted toward mid-2026; earlier anchors (CoRL 2025, ICLR 2026) included as venue markers.*

*Method: mined the workspace Hugging Face Daily Papers dumps (`data/hf_ranked.json`, `data/hf_daily_full.json`, covering 2025-10-01 → 2026-10-01, 472 embodied/robotics titles) and the scraped conference tables (`pc_iclr_2026.jsonl`, `pc_corl_2025.jsonl`), then fetched arXiv abstract pages, full HTML for key papers, and lab/conference pages via curl. Hugging Face upvotes are used only as an attention signal, not as evidence of quality. All fetched content is treated as untrusted data.*

## 1. Synthesis — what is genuinely happening

**The field has placed a single big bet: the generalist embodied foundation model, and by mid-2026 that bet has become a data-and-systems race rather than an architecture race.** Almost every serious release in the window claims the same shape — one VLA/policy trained across many tasks, environments and robot bodies, adapted with prompt conditioning or light fine-tuning. Qwen-VLA unifies manipulation, vision-and-language navigation and trajectory prediction in one model with embodiment-aware prompts ([2605.30280](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30280)); Qwen-RobotManip and Qwen-RobotNav push the same "alignment unlocks scale" argument for manipulation and navigation separately ([2606.17846](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17846), [2606.18112](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18112)); Xiaomi-Robotics-1 trains on **100k+ hours** of real UMI trajectories and reports monotone data/model scaling ([2607.15330](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15330)); Being-H0.5 claims **35,000 hours across 30 embodiments** ([2601.12993](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12993)); Green-VLA layers a five-stage curriculum on a humanoid ([2602.00919](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00919)); Tencent's HY-Embodied-0.5, Ant's LingBot-VLA, RynnBrain 1.1 and GigaBrain-0.5 fill out the same slot. On the closed frontier, Google DeepMind's **Gemini Robotics 2** (30 Jul 2026) controls a full humanoid "feet to fingertips", adds multi-robot teamwork and a few-hours on-device adaptation path; Figure's **Helix 02** (27 Jan 2026) demonstrates a single visuomotor network doing a four-minute autonomous dishwasher cycle with a learned whole-body controller (System 0) trained on >1,000 h of human motion plus sim-to-real RL; NVIDIA's **GR00T N1.7** is billed as commercially deployable and **GR00T N2** was teased for end-2026 with a claimed ">2x success in novel environments". The strategic reading: *unified generalist models are winning the narrative*, but the credible evidence is concentrated in a handful of well-resourced labs, and the open literature is now dominated by Chinese industrial teams (Qwen, Xiaomi, Ant, Tencent, AgiBot, GigaBrain, Green/NeoteAI) publishing open weights or detailed reports.

**The second real shift is the post-training stack: RL and world models moved from side-quests to the default recipe.** π*0.6 (Physical Intelligence) is the clearest statement — RECAP, an advantage-conditioned RL method that mixes autonomous rollouts, teleoperated interventions and reward feedback, more than doubles throughput and roughly halves failures on espresso, laundry and box assembly in real settings ([2511.14759](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14759)). A parallel open wave (SimpleVLA-RL, π_RL for flow-based VLAs, VLA-RFT, RLinf, RIPT-VLA) applies RLVR-style pipelines to continuous control, and world-model-based RL now appears in GigaBrain-0.5M* ([2602.12099](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12099)) and the WCM world-critic line. On the modelling side, "World-Action Models" (WAMs) crystallised as a named paradigm: **two independent surveys** appeared within four months ([2605.12090](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12090), [2609.16074](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16074)), and NVIDIA's **Cosmos 3** explicitly sets out to subsume VLM, video generator, world simulator and world-action model in one omnimodal mixture-of-transformers with open weights ([2606.02800](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02800)). Autonomous driving followed the same path: Qwen-Drive-1.0 adds an explicit BEV 3D-perception probe and a flow-matching planner to a frozen VLM architecture for 90.7 PDMS on NAVSIM ([2609.00111](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00111)); NVIDIA released the open reasoning-VLA **Alpamayo** family with AlpaSim and Physical AI datasets (Jan 2026); Wayve's **GAIA-4** pitches a closed-loop "Simulation 2.0" world model for safety cases; SimWAM uses future-video prediction only as training-time supervision to avoid inference cost ([2608.07468](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07468)). A third data trend is worth isolating: **robot-free human video is being treated as the scaling substrate** — Dyna Robotics' DYNA-2 is reported as pre-trained on ~1M hours of first-person human video with no robot data and 80–90% success in precision assembly, Ego2Robot synthesises 18,561 h of robot data from ego video across 15 morphologies, and Open-AoE ships ~2,000 h of smartphone-captured manipulation.

**The third real shift is that evaluation became a first-class research contribution — and it is unforgiving.** The year's most useful robotics paper is not a model: "What Are We Actually Benchmarking in Robot Manipulation?" shows LIBERO is *shortcut-solvable* (a 0.09B DINOv2+MLP probe with no language encoder lands within ~1 point of reported SOTA), that only **19.8% of LIBERO SOTA claims are provably statistically significant**, that CALVIN's ATC falls 4.17→3.14 when block poses are resampled *inside* the training distribution, and that a 22M task-specific policy matches a 0.9B foundation VLA on SimplerEnv by training next to the test ([2606.04233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04233)). LIBERO-Plus had already shown SOTA collapsing from ~95% to below 30% under modest viewpoint/initial-state perturbations and models largely ignoring language ([2510.13626](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13626)); LIBERO-Para showed 22–52 pp drops under paraphrase ([2603.28301](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28301)); Act2Answer and HumanCLAW attack VLA/VLM knowledge and action-decision confounds; RoboArena pushes distributed real-world evaluation. **Is the field scaling or fragmenting?** Both, and the tension is the story: the *model* side is consolidating hard on unified generalist policies (clear scaling), while the *evaluation and data* side is fragmenting so badly that most reported benchmark gains cannot be trusted as evidence of capability. The honest 2026 summary is: real capability progress on long-horizon, contact-rich, whole-body tasks by a few labs; a genuine, well-validated RL-and-world-model post-training wave; and a benchmark literature that has largely failed its own validity checks.

## 2. Ranked papers (18)

**1. Qwen-VLA: Unifying Vision-Language-Action Modeling across Tasks, Environments, and Robot Embodiments** — Qiuyue Wang et al. (Qwen Team, Alibaba) — arXiv [2605.30280](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30280) — arXiv preprint, 28 May 2026. Extends the Qwen VLM stack to continuous action via a DiT action decoder and jointly pretrains on manipulation trajectories, egocentric human demos, simulation, VLN data and vision-language data, with embodiment-aware textual prompts. **Evidence:** a single instruct model reports 97.9% LIBERO, 73.7% Simpler-WidowX, 86.1/87.2% RoboTwin-Easy/Hard, 69.0 OSR R2R, 59.6 SR RxR, 76.9% average real-world OOD success on ALOHA, and 26.6% zero-shot on DOMINO. **Why it matters:** it is the clearest open demonstration that manipulation, navigation and trajectory prediction can share one policy — the "consolidation" thesis in a single artifact. Caveat: driving/trajectory benchmarks are largely its own regime, and I did not see independent replication.

**2. Gemini Robotics 2 (with Gemini Robotics ER 2 and On-Device 2)** — Google DeepMind (Carolina Parada et al.) — blog: [deepmind.google](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-brings-whole-body-intelligence-to-robots/) — 30 Jul 2026. A three-model family: a whole-body VLA from feet to fingertips across Apollo 2 humanoids and Franka Duo, an embodied-reasoning VLM for multi-minute plans and human dialogue, and an efficient on-device VLA that adapts to new embodiments in "a few hours". **Evidence:** the post itself reports medium-to-high success on whole-body and gripper tasks, explicitly admits multi-finger dexterity "remains challenging", and shows the same checkpoint across three embodiments. **Why it matters:** it is the strongest first-party evidence that whole-body loco-manipulation and cross-embodiment transfer are now engineering problems, not science demos. Caveat: no paper, no per-task numbers, early-access weights only.

**3. π*0.6: a VLA That Learns From Experience** — Physical Intelligence (Ali Amin et al.) — arXiv [2511.14759](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14759) — 18 Nov 2025. Introduces RECAP: value-function training plus advantage-conditioned policy extraction that ingests demonstrations, on-policy autonomous data and expert interventions, enabling offline-iterated RL of a flow-based VLA. **Evidence:** on the hardest tasks throughput more than doubles and failure rate roughly halves; 90%+ success on espresso making, diverse laundry folding and box assembly; ablations separate offline RL, on-robot data and intervention contributions. **Why it matters:** it is the most credible published argument that real-world RL post-training — not more imitation data — is the path to reliability. Caveat: the paper itself states the loop is not autonomous (human reward labels, interventions, resets) and exploration is largely greedy.

**4. What Are We Actually Benchmarking in Robot Manipulation?** — Tianchong Jiang et al. — arXiv [2606.04233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04233) — 2 Jun 2026, with released diagnostics. Audits LIBERO, CALVIN, SimplerEnv, RoboCasa and RoboTwin 2.0 across four failure modes: shortcut solvability, missing statistical significance, creeping overfitting, and data-source dependence. **Evidence:** a 0.09B language-free DINOv2+MLP probe is within ~1 pt of best reported LIBERO scores; only 19.8%/19.7% of LIBERO/SimplerEnv SOTA claims are provably significant; CALVIN ATC drops 4.17→3.14 under in-distribution pose resampling; a 22M SimplerEnv policy reaches 94.8% vs X-VLA's 95.8% by training beside the test. **Why it matters:** it invalidates the field's most-cited progress proxy and gives reviewers reusable diagnostics. **Read in full.**

**5. Cosmos 3: Omnimodal World Models for Physical AI** — NVIDIA (Agarwal et al.) — arXiv [2606.02800](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02800) — 1 Jun 2026, open weights under OpenMDW-1.1. A mixture-of-transformers world model over language, image, video, audio and action that unified reasoner and generator towers and was post-trained into text-to-image, image-to-video and robot-policy variants. **Evidence:** ranked best open-source T2I/I2V by Artificial Analysis and best policy model on RoboArena at the time of writing; weights, code, synthetic datasets and a benchmark released. **Why it matters:** the strongest single bet that "world model" is one foundation-model problem spanning simulation, generation and control; open weights make it the reference baseline.

**6. Qwen-Drive-1.0: An Initial Step towards a Vision-Language Foundation Model for Autonomous Driving** — Qwen Team (Xin Zhou et al.) — arXiv [2609.00111](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00111) — 31 Aug 2026, open 4B weights. Keeps the pretrained VLM architecture intact and attaches an external BEV head (3D detection + occupancy + map segmentation) and a flow-matching Planning Expert, trained in four stages with mixed driving and general VLM data. **Evidence:** 90.7 PDMS on NAVSIM v1.1, RFS 7.91 on WOD-E2E test, competitive 3D perception, and stated preservation of general VLM ability. **Why it matters:** it is the most complete open end-to-end driving foundation model, with an inspectable 3D interface. Caveat (from its own limitations): reasoning traces do not always match the executed trajectory, and the immediate 1–2 s decision can contradict the stated cause.

**7. Xiaomi-Robotics-1: Scaling VLA Models with over 100K Hours of Real-World Trajectories** — Xiaomi Robotics Team (Jun Guo et al.) — arXiv [2607.15330](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15330) — 16 Jul 2026. Two-stage recipe using UMI-style capture and a scalable auto-labelling pipeline that generates natural-language descriptions of state transitions, followed by embodiment alignment for imperative instructions. **Evidence:** reports consistent improvement with data scale and model size and out-of-the-box mobile manipulation in unseen environments. **Why it matters:** if the scaling claim holds, it is the clearest statement that robot learning is now data-limited and that auto-labelled real trajectories are the bottleneck resource. Caveat: proprietary data, no public benchmark protocol yet.

**8. GEN-0 / GEN-1** — Generalist AI (Generalist Team) — blog: [generalistai.com](https://generalistai.com/blog/accelerating-the-next-phase-of-physical-ai) — GEN-0 Nov 2025; GEN-1 April 2026; $400M raise announced 4 Jun 2026. Claims the first robotics pretraining scaling laws from real-world data at scale (GEN-0), then commercial-threshold reliability (GEN-1): 99% reliability on diverse tasks, up to 3x faster execution than prior SOTA, and emergent improvisation. **Why it matters:** it is the load-bearing industry claim that "scaling laws exist in robotics", the analogue of the LLM narrative. Caveat: **not peer-reviewed, no paper, no data or weights released** — treat the scaling-law claim as unverified; the cited figures are company-reported.

**9. Introducing Helix 02: Full-Body Autonomy** — Figure AI — blog: [figure.ai](https://www.figure.ai/news/helix-02) — 27 Jan 2026. A single visuomotor network mapping all onboard sensors (vision, touch, proprioception) to all actuators, organised as System 2 (slow reasoning), System 1 (200 Hz full-body targets) and a new System 0 (1 kHz balance/contact controller trained on >1,000 h human motion + sim-to-real RL, replacing ~109,504 lines of C++). **Evidence:** a continuous four-minute autonomous kitchen/dishwasher loco-manipulation run with no resets, plus new tactile dexterity (pill extraction, syringe dosing, singulation). **Why it matters:** it is the most concrete demonstration that humanoid locomotion and manipulation can be one learned system rather than stitched controllers. Caveat: demo video, no quantitative evaluation, no independent reproduction.

**10. X-VLA: Soft-Prompted Transformer as Scalable Cross-Embodiment VLA** — Jinliang Zheng et al. — arXiv [2510.10274](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10274) — ICLR 2026 (Poster). Adds one learnable soft-prompt embedding set per data source (embodiment) to a flow-matching VLA, so heterogeneous robot data can be trained jointly with minimal new parameters and no embodiment-specific towers. **Evidence:** ICLR 2026 acceptance; 0.9B model reported at or above much larger VLAs across cross-embodiment suites. **Why it matters:** it made "cheap cross-embodiment conditioning" a default design and is one of the most-cited baselines in the audit paper (where its SimplerEnv score is matched by a 22M policy). 

**11. SimpleVLA-RL: Scaling VLA Training via Reinforcement Learning** — Haozhan Li et al. — arXiv [2509.09674](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09674) — ICLR 2026. Adapts RL-with-verifiable-rewards-style training to VLA action planning, using rollout scaling and exploration to escape imitation ceilings. **Evidence:** ICLR 2026 acceptance; it anchors the open RL-for-VLA wave alongside π_RL and RIPT-VLA. **Why it matters:** it is the open-weights counterpart to π*0.6's industrial claim that RL, not more SFT, is the post-training lever.

**12. LIBERO-Plus: In-depth Robustness Analysis of Vision-Language-Action Models** — Senyu Fei et al. — arXiv [2510.13626](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13626) — 15 Oct 2025 (v3 Dec 2025). Systematic single- and multi-dimension perturbations across object layout, camera viewpoint, robot initial state, language, lighting, background and sensor noise, plus a compositional-generalisation gap analysis. **Evidence:** performances fall from ~95% to below 30% under modest camera/initial-state shifts; models are largely insensitive to language variation, and follow-up probing suggests they often ignore instructions entirely. **Why it matters:** the canonical "high LIBERO score ≠ competence" result that the 2026 audit then formalised.

**13. Being-H0.5: Scaling Human-Centric Robot Learning for Cross-Embodiment Generalization** — Hao Luo et al. — arXiv [2601.12993](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12993) — 19 Jan 2026. Proposes treating human interaction traces as a universal "mother tongue", with UniHand-2.0 (>35,000 h, 30 embodiments) and a Unified Action Space that maps heterogeneous controls into semantically aligned slots. **Evidence:** largest claimed embodied pretraining recipe to date; cross-embodiment gains from human-data bootstrapping. **Why it matters:** it is the strongest open statement of the human-video-first data thesis. Caveat: dataset is proprietary in practice and the recipe has not been publicly reproduced.

**14. RoboCasa365: A Large-Scale Simulation Framework for Training and Benchmarking Generalist Robots** — Soroush Nasiriany et al. — arXiv [2603.04356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04356) — 4 Mar 2026; ICLR 2026 (datasets & benchmarks). 365 household mobile-manipulation tasks over 2,500 kitchen scenes with 600 h human and 1,600 h synthetic demonstrations, designed for reproducible large-scale evaluation. **Why it matters:** it is the main attempt to answer the audit's call for broad-variation benchmarks — and the audit paper itself finds RoboCasa among the benchmarks that fail the fewest diagnostics. Caveat: simulation-only, and synthetic-data-heavy.

**15. World-Action Models surveys** — Zuxing Lu et al. — arXiv [2609.16074](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16074) (13 Sep 2026, MBZUAI/Caltech/GaTech/NYU/Berkeley); and Siyin Wang et al. — arXiv [2605.12090](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12090) (12 May 2026). Both formalise WAMs — models that couple future-state prediction with executable action generation — and taxonomise them (cascaded vs joint; representations, transition modelling, action interfaces, data and scaling). The 2026 survey additionally reviews manipulation, navigation and driving applications. **Why it matters:** two independent surveys in one year is the standard signal that a paradigm has crystallised; they are the best entry point to the subfield. Caveat: the companion survey *Do World Models Make Better Robots?* (arXiv [2609.29669](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29669)) catalogs 160 benchmarks and concludes the field **cannot yet demonstrate a closed-loop advantage of world modelling over direct VLA policies** — a direct caution on the WAM narrative.

**16. Green-VLA: Staged Vision-Language-Action Model for Generalist Robots** — I. Apanasevich et al. — arXiv [2602.00919](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00919) — 31 Jan 2026. Five-stage curriculum (foundational VLM → multimodal grounding → multi-embodiment pretraining → embodiment adaptation → RL policy alignment), 3,000 h of demonstrations with temporal alignment/quality filtering, and a unified embodiment-aware action interface for humanoids, mobile manipulators and fixed arms. **Evidence:** highest-upvoted embodied Hugging Face Daily Paper in the window (322 upvotes). **Why it matters:** it is emblematic of the industrial VLA recipe now standardised across Chinese and European robotics firms. Caveat: corporate report for the "Green" humanoid with limited independent evaluation; the upvote signal is attention, not validation.

**17. SimWAM: A Simple World Action Model for End-to-End Autonomous Driving** — Zongchuang Zhao et al. — arXiv [2608.07468](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07468) — 7 Aug 2026. Uses future-video prediction only as a training-time supervision signal, co-training a frozen video expert and a lightweight action expert with joint flow matching and an isolated attention mask, so inference needs no future-frame generation; adds compositional RL beyond imitation. **Evidence:** 91.9 PDMS on NAVSIM with favourable accuracy/compute trade-off. **Why it matters:** it is the cleanest response to the central WAM objection — that coupling prediction to action is too slow for real-time driving.

**18. GigaBrain-0.5M*: a VLA That Learns From World Model-Based Reinforcement Learning** — GigaBrain Team (Boyuan Wang et al.) — arXiv [2602.12099](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12099) — 12 Feb 2026. Built on GigaBrain-0.5 (10,000+ h manipulation pretraining) and post-trained with world-model-based RL, using the video world model as both reward source and imagination rollout. **Evidence:** the intermediate GigaBrain-0.5 is reported first on the RoboChallenge benchmark, and the companion GigaBrain-0 line uses world-model-generated data to cut real-robot data needs. **Why it matters:** it fuses the two mid-2026 post-training themes — world models and RL — into one recipe, and anchors the leaderboard-driven evaluation culture.

**Scaling vs fragmentation verdict:** models are scaling and consolidating (one unified policy, more embodiments, more data, more modalities); data and evaluation are fragmenting, with no benchmark surviving audit and no shared real-world task suite. That asymmetry — capability centralising, measurement decentralising — is the defining structural feature of the field in this window.

## 5. Caveats

- **The largest results in this area are not papers.** Gemini Robotics 2, Figure Helix 02, GEN-0/GEN-1, DYNA-2, GR00T N1.7/N2 and Alpamayo were announced via blogs/press releases with no peer review, no per-task data and often no weights. I treated them as important capability signals but not as verified evidence. The GEN-0 "scaling laws in robotics" claim in particular is unverifiable from the public record.
- **Hugging Face upvotes measure attention, not quality.** Green-VLA's 322 upvotes (the top embodied paper) reflect a corporate release with limited independent evaluation; several high-upvote papers are technical reports. I used upvotes only for ranking salience.
- **The benchmark crisis applies to my own ranked list.** LIBERO appears as evidence in entries 1, 3 (training), 10 and others, yet the audit paper shows LIBERO is shortcut-solvable and statistically underpowered, and that X-VLA can be matched by a 22M policy on SimplerEnv. Any "SOTA on LIBERO/RoboTwin/SimplerEnv" figure in this document should be read with that discount. I have flagged this where it matters.
- **Single-benchmark and self-reported leaderboard claims.** GigaBrain's RoboChallenge rank, Cosmos 3's RoboArena rank and Qwen-VLA's DOMINO/RoboCasa numbers are self- or sponsor-reported on benchmarks that are not independently audited. Treat cross-lab comparisons as unreliable.
- **World-model-for-control remains an unproven closed-loop advantage.** The benchmark survey [2609.29669](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29669) explicitly concludes the field cannot yet show that world modelling beats direct VLA policies at closed-loop task success; the "world models understand physics" framing is further punctured by the object-permanence results reported elsewhere in this review program (WROP, [2609.28654](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28654)).
- **Reasoning-trace faithfulness is weak.** Qwen-Drive's own limitations state that planned trajectories do not always follow the textual rationale and that immediate 1–2 s decisions can contradict the stated cause; the "Deferred Exposure" paper ([2608.01755](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01755)) shows driving CoT supervision leaks ground-truth trajectories and induces rationalisation rather than inference. Driving-reasoning gains may be partly conditioning artifacts.
- **Data provenance is opaque.** The strongest scaling claims (Xiaomi 100k h, Being-H0.5 35k h, Dyna-2 1M h) rest on proprietary or only partly released corpora, so the central "data scale → capability" claim cannot be independently reproduced.
- **Conference coverage is partial in the workspace data.** ICLR 2026 was fully scraped (19,809 entries) and CoRL 2025 (263), but the ICML 2026 and CVPR 2026 scrapes are small fragments (418 / 241 entries with mostly anonymised titles), so I could not do reliable venue-level topic counts for those two. I therefore leaned on the ICLR 2026 and CoRL 2025 numbers plus HF attention. The CVPR 2026 award I could verify ("Risk-Controllable Multi-View Diffusion for Driving Scenario Generation") is a *workshop* (VGBE) best paper, not a main-conference award; ICML 2026's headline awards contained no robotics/embodied paper.
- **Late-window truncation.** HF data ends 2026-10-01 and arXiv metadata for the final days of the window is thin; September–October 2026 items are likely under-sampled.
- **Language coverage.** A large share of the industrial VLA output is announced first in Chinese-language media (tencent, 36kr, GTC coverage); I used those sources where no English paper existed and flagged them.
